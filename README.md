# 🧠 MemoryMate

### AI Meeting Intelligence Agent powered by Hindsight

MemoryMate is an AI meeting intelligence agent that remembers information across meetings and uses that accumulated memory to prepare users for future conversations.

Instead of treating every meeting as a new conversation, MemoryMate uses **Hindsight as a persistent memory layer** to remember priorities, preferences, concerns, deadlines, and commitments.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Professionals often have repeated meetings with the same customers, clients, partners, or stakeholders.

Important information can be scattered across previous meetings:

- What does the customer care about?
- What problems did they mention?
- What did we promise?
- What deadlines were discussed?
- How do they prefer to communicate?

Manually searching through old meeting notes before every meeting is time-consuming.

MemoryMate solves this problem by allowing an AI agent to remember relevant information from previous interactions and use it when preparing for the next meeting.

---

## 💡 Solution

MemoryMate stores meeting information in Hindsight and uses the accumulated memories to generate a personalized meeting briefing.

The workflow is:

```text
Meeting Notes
      ↓
Hindsight Retain
      ↓
Persistent Memory
      ↓
Hindsight Recall / Reflect
      ↓
Meeting Intelligence
      ↓
Personalized Meeting Preparation
